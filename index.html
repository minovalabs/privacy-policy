Privacy Policy

Minova Cashbook — Personal Finance Tracker

Effective Date: March 11, 2026 Last Updated: March 11, 2026 App: Minova Cashbook Platform: Android

01 Introduction

Minova Cashbook ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal finance and cash tracking application built to help you manage your daily income and expenses.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it. By using Minova Cashbook, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

02 Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Google profile picture

This is used solely to identify your account and personalize your experience.

b. Financial Data You Enter

All transaction records you create manually, including:

  • Income and expense amounts
  • Categories and descriptions
  • Transaction dates and notes
Your data belongs to you. This information is stored in your personal account and is never sold, shared, or used commercially.

c. Technical & Device Information

We may collect limited technical data to maintain the app, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version number
  • Crash reports and error logs

03 How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate and maintain your account securely
  • Store and sync your financial records across devices
  • Provide, maintain, and improve app features
  • Diagnose bugs and resolve technical issues
We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, machine learning, or any commercial purposes.

04 Data Storage & Security

Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure.

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS / TLS
  • All data is encrypted at rest by Firebase
  • Access is restricted to your authenticated account only
  • No employee or third party can access your personal financial data

06 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties, with the sole exception of:

  • When required by applicable law or legal process
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of users or the public

07 Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account remains active. If you choose to delete your account, all associated personal data and financial records will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

08 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — View all your personal data within the app at any time
  • Correction — Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export — Request a copy of your data at any time
  • Portability — Transfer your data to another service

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 11.

09 Children's Privacy

Minova Cashbook is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.
